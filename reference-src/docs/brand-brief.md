# Brand Brief for the App (brand-neutral)

Source: the uploaded brand files. Names, hashtags and numbers tied to the brand stay in private config, not in code.

- **What it is:** an AI coffee brand where people set taste preferences and get custom bean ratios and brewing recipes. USP: freshness, a 72-hour roast-to-delivery promise, guaranteed for **all bean types** (decision, Oct 2026).
- **Niche (decision, Oct 2026):** coffee addicts who keep looking for something special. See `audience-and-strategy-2026-12.md`.
- **Primary audiences (decision):** the four personas only: a student who wants focus coffee (18), a content creator who wants packaging and scheduled delivery (23), a home-based mom who wants affordable, gentle, quick coffee (28), and a startup founder who wants prestige, subscriptions and 24/7 delivery (35). Older users are no longer primary, but large text, captions and contrast stay on for everyone.
- **Tone:** warm, funny, simple, Egyptian colloquial; humor lands best with younger users (78% Reels engagement in the files); technology is explained like a "coffee-savvy friend".
- **Visual identity (config only):** yellow background, green text, red accents; bean-silhouette and circuit-line patterns. Main font: **Felfel** (licensed, headline-oriented Ruq'ah style), with Cairo for body text and small sizes.
- **Themes:** freshness proof, bean's journey, community blends, generational warmth, Hadota's harmless bloopers.
- **Goals:** app downloads, UGC, freshness association. Dates re-based to start December 2026 (see content plan).
- **Claims:** may state the 72-hour freshness promise. Must not state patent status (files conflict: "patented" vs "patent-pending"), health benefits, or unsourced statistics.
