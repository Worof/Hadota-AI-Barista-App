# Revised marketing strategy (from December 2026): coffee addicts who want something special

Brand-neutral. Persona facts come from the uploaded persona deck; everything marked *hypothesis* must be validated (plan in section 7). No market statistics are quoted here because none were verified for Egypt in this pass.

## 1. What changes
- **Niche, not mass market.** The product and the community (قهوة الحكواتية) are for heavy, enthusiastic coffee drinkers ("addicts") who keep looking for a cup they cannot buy elsewhere. Casual drinkers and price-only shoppers are not the target; older, low-tech users are no longer primary.
- **The community is the moat.** Anyone can sell beans. Few can offer a place where addicts swap stories, take weekly challenges and have a barista (Hadota) who learns their taste.
- **Personalization is the product.** Each rating teaches Hadota (a per-user contextual bandit), so the blend gets closer over time.

## 3. "Something special they cannot find anywhere else"
1. **A blend made for me**, learned from my own ratings (competitors sell fixed blends).
2. **Rare beans and origins**: all four species (Arabica, Robusta, Liberica, Excelsa) from 20+ countries, including bright Ethiopians, Yemeni heirloom and smoky Liberica that chains rarely show.
3. **Proof of freshness**: roast and deliver within 72 hours for every bean type (competitors make no freshness claim).
4. **Belonging**: a coffeehouse for storytellers, with a weekly challenge.
5. **Craft knowledge**: guides on flavor, acidity and brewing (competitors publish little or none).

## 4. Consumption occasions to speak to
Morning ritual, focus and study sessions, creative breaks, client and office hospitality, family time, after-iftar in Ramadan (expected around 8-9 Feb 2027, confirm), gifting at Eid and Mother's Day.

## 6. Plan Dec 2026 to Mar 2027 (cadence from the distribution plan)
- **Dec:** launch the community and Hadota; "meet the four beans" series; invite-a-fellow-addict referrals; weekly challenge every Thursday.
- **Jan:** focus and creator content (Khaled, Rana); office subscription pilot (Ahmed); family low-acid guide (Laila).

- **KPIs (set baselines in week 1):** names entered, community joins, ratings per user (training data), repeat blends, weekly challenge posts, referral invites sent, 72-hour delivery success. Targets to be agreed once baselines exist.

## 7. How we learn the audience (research plan)
| Hypothesis | How to test | When |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy drinkers (3+ cups) want rare origins more than low price | In-app cups/day vs. origin choices; 5-8 interviews per persona | Dec-Jan |
| Persona-specific unmet needs in section 2 are real | Interviews + a short survey in the community | Dec |
| Ratings improve repeat blends | Compare ratings of first vs. later blends per user | From week 4 |
| Community challenges drive return visits | Weekly active users, challenge posts | From week 2 |
First-party data stays on the device until a server and consent flow exist; do not collect more than needed.

## 8. Risks
Community cold start (no shared server yet), sourcing and stock for "all bean types" at 72-hour delivery, conflicting patent claims in the brand files, voice quality until studio clips are rendered, and the persona inconsistencies above.
